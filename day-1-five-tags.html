Dispatch note: 8 HTML tags

Each row below uses one new tag in a real sentence from a freight office. View the page source to see how it was written.

<abbr>

The driver confirms the ETA by phone, and the client signs the POD on receipt.

Hover over the dotted words to read the full meaning.

<mark>

Parcels for Mombasa leave at 6 am. Late bookings are not accepted after 5 pm.

<time>

Next departure: Monday, 2 November 2026 at 6:00 am.

The text can be friendly; the datetime value stays in a fixed format.

<blockquote> and <cite>

Amateurs talk strategy. Professionals talk logistics.

A saying often attributed to military circles, quoted in supply chain training courses.

<code>

To make text bold, wrap it like this: <strong>Fragile</strong>. Write &lt; to show a less-than sign on the page.

<details> and <summary>

What goes on a waybill?

Sender, receiver, item description, weight, and the delivery address.

What if a parcel is damaged?

Photograph it at delivery, note it on the POD, and report it the same day.
